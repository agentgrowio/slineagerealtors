You Have Visited The Omaxe State: What to Ask Before You Decide

After your visit to The Omaxe State, Dwarka: what to read in Omaxe's agreement, what to ask about the cost sheet, how to check RERA and paperwork questions.

“We have seen The Omaxe State. What should we ask before we decide?”

It is a good question to ask, and it is fine to take your time over it. The plain answer: a visit shows you the place, and the details that matter are in three documents. Those are Omaxe's agreement, the current official cost sheet for your unit, and the project's Delhi RERA registration, DLRERA2024P0003.

This page is the next step after your visit, or before one you have booked. It covers what to read, what to ask me, what to settle at home, how to check RERA yourself and what to ask about the paperwork, from booking to the lease agreement. If you live in Dwarka or West Delhi, or work in Gurugram, we can go through any of it in person.

What should you read in Omaxe's agreement?

At The Omaxe State, the rental terms are as per Omaxe's agreement. I do not quote rent figures or percentages, because a number said out loud is worth nothing if the agreement says something else. I show you the agreement terms in writing, and we read them together.

These are the points I go through with you. Write the answers down as you read:

  1. When the rental arrangement starts. From which event or date does it apply to your unit?
  2. How long it runs. What is the term, and what happens when it ends?
  3. How the rent changes. Does it change over the term, and on what basis?
  4. Who pays which charges. Maintenance, common area charges and utilities: whose cost is each one?
  5. What happens if the space falls vacant. If a tenant leaves, what does the agreement say happens next?
  6. Who you deal with. Who pays you, how, and whom do you contact if there is a problem?

If the agreement does not answer one of these clearly, ask before you book, and get the answer in writing. For a longer explanation of the word itself, read What Pre-Leased Means: A Plain Guide for Dwarka Buyers.

What should you ask in a cost-sheet walkthrough?

Units start from about ₹37.5 lakh. That is the starting point, not the price of every unit. The price of a specific unit is on the current official cost sheet, and I walk you through it line by line, free of charge.

A walkthrough works best when you lead it with your own questions. Here are the ones worth asking me:

  • Is this the current official cost sheet, and for which exact unit?
  • Which line is the price of the unit?
  • What does each line on this sheet mean, in plain words?
  • Is everything I will be asked to pay written on this sheet? If not, where is it written?
  • Which unit type is this: a retail shop, a food court unit or an office unit?

Please do not rely on a figure you heard in passing, from me or from anyone else. If it matters to you, ask to see it on the cost sheet.

A good decision is one you can explain from the documents, line by line, to the people who matter to you.

What should you settle with your family before deciding?

If others in your family will share this decision, it helps to talk through a few things at home before the next conversation with me. That way, everyone who decides has seen the same documents.

  • What you want from the purchase. Income beside a salary, something for retirement, an asset in Delhi while you live abroad, or a shop to run yourself. Tell me plainly, and I will tell you plainly what fits.
  • Who will read the agreement. Decide who goes through Omaxe's agreement and the cost sheet with me, and bring them to the walkthrough or the call.
  • Whose name the unit will be in. If that raises a tax or legal question, I can connect you with the right professional.
  • How you will pay. If you need a loan, or want to understand the tax side of buying, I can connect you with the right professional. I do not give loan or tax advice myself.

There is no need to settle all of this in one evening. Talk it over at home and come back to me with what is still unclear. I am happy to go through the documents again with anyone in your family.

How do you check the RERA registration yourself?

The project is registered with Delhi RERA under DLRERA2024P0003. RERA is the Real Estate Regulatory Authority, the body with which real estate projects in Delhi are registered. You do not have to take my word for it:

  1. Open rera.delhi.gov.in, the Delhi RERA website.
  2. Look up the registration number DLRERA2024P0003.
  3. Check that the project name and the promoter match what you are being shown.
  4. Note the timeline registered there. That is the timeline registered for the project.

Of the three documents, the RERA registration is the one that is public. The cost sheet and Omaxe's agreement are documents I go through with you directly. For the basic facts about the project, see The Omaxe State, Dwarka: Price, RERA, Unit Types, Plain Answers.

What happens on paper, from booking to the lease agreement?

I help you with the paperwork, from booking through to the lease agreement, and we read each document together before you sign it. I do not list the steps here, because I go through the exact documents for your unit, and their order, with you before you begin.

These are the questions worth asking me before you book:

  2. Can I read each document, in plain words, before I sign it?
  3. Which documents should I keep ready for the booking?
  4. Where are the rental terms written, and when do I see them in writing?
