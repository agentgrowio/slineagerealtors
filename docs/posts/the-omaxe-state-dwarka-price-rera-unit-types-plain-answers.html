What is The Omaxe State, and who is building it?

The Omaxe State is a 50.4-acre, stadium-led project in Sector 19B, Dwarka, New Delhi.

It is a project of Omaxe with the DDA (Delhi Development Authority), set up as a public-private partnership. In a public-private partnership, a public body and a private company each take on a part of a project. That is a description of how the project is structured. It is not a promise about rent or price.

Launch coverage in September 2024 described planned districts for sports, shopping, hospitality, food and social life. Close by are Yashobhoomi, the India International Convention and Expo Centre in Dwarka, and the airport corridor.

Is it RERA registered, and how do I check?

Yes. The project is registered with Delhi RERA under DLRERA2024P0003. RERA is the Real Estate Regulatory Authority, the body with which real estate projects in Delhi are registered.

You do not have to take my word for it. You can check it yourself in a few steps:

  1. Open rera.delhi.gov.in, the Delhi RERA website.
  2. Look up the registration number DLRERA2024P0003.
  3. Check that the project name and the promoter match what you are being shown.
  4. Note the timeline registered there. That is the timeline registered for the project.

Of everything on this page, the RERA registration is the part that is public. The cost sheet and Omaxe's agreement are documents I go through with you directly.

What does “pre-leased” mean here?

With an ordinary shop, you buy the space and then find a tenant yourself. With a pre-leased unit, the leasing is arranged as part of the purchase, so you are buying a property and an income arrangement together.

At The Omaxe State, the rental terms are as per Omaxe's agreement. When the arrangement starts, how long it runs and how the rent works are all in that agreement, and I show you the terms in writing before you book. I do not quote rent figures or percentages, because the agreement is what counts.

A common question is: is my rent guaranteed? The honest answer is that “pre-leased” on its own does not tell you that. The agreement tells you what applies. I have written a longer explanation in What Pre-Leased Means: A Plain Guide for Dwarka Buyers.
