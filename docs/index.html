Before you book

Five questions to ask any seller, me included

Whoever you buy from, these are worth asking. I will answer each of them with documents.

  1. Is the project registered? Look up DLRERA2024P0003 on rera.delhi.gov.in and match the promoter and project name.
  2. Where is the rent written down? Ask to see the rental terms in the agreement itself, not only in a brochure or a message.
  3. What is in the price? Ask for the current official cost sheet for the exact unit, floor and payment plan.
  4. Which timeline applies? The one registered with Delhi RERA. Be careful with any date that is not.
  5. Who is the seller? Ask how they are connected to the developer. I am an authorised channel partner, not Omaxe.
